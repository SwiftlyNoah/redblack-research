## Style sheet (2026-08-04 restructure; mandatory for all new prose)

- No em dashes in prose (`---` only in comments/table placeholders);
  prefer commas, periods, parentheses.
- Banned constructions: the "not X; it is Y" antithesis tic,
  colon-then-elaboration as a default, rhetorical section titles,
  road-map paragraphs beyond one in the intro, re-previews/recaps,
  self-referential "laboratory" talk, and project-management vocabulary
  (ship gate, window, ledger, generation) in paper prose.
- The `incident` environment is ABOLISHED. Measured lessons live as
  dated `\begin{remark}[Title, YYYY-MM]` environments. The
  never-delete-never-soften rule still applies to their substance
  (e.g. Paper III's Remark "A rejected certificate" keeps the full
  1.958 story).
- Definitions strictly before use; every paper defines or explicitly
  imports each symbol it uses (Paper III now has its own Preliminaries
  with TV, eps_G, the probe family, CBV).
- Paper I's organizing spine is the four-rung state ladder (Sec. 3);
  new Paper I content should name its rung. G' is formally Paper I
  Def. 10.6; cite that, do not redefine.
- Renumbering map applied in this pass: Paper I Thm 3.7 -> 4.7,
  11.1 -> 10.1, 11.2 -> 10.2, 11.3 -> 10.3, 11.4 -> 10.4. Paper II's
  results moved to Sec. 6 (LBR/TV/distance in 6.5, suicide theorem
  Thm 6.1). Paper III cites Paper II section-free by design.
- The "52.83% four-card root" figure was removed from Paper II
  (2026-08-04): no script generates it and Paper I never contained it.
  Do not reintroduce it without a script.

The papers' house rules:

1. **No number without a script.** Every numeral is regenerated by
   `state_counts.py`, `real_game_size.py`, `verify_against_engine.py`,
   or (for solver measurements) the scripts under
   `simulator/scripts/` in the game repo, whose JSON artifacts live in
   `simulator/results/solver/`. If you add a number, name its script in
   the text or the Verification section.
2. **Plain dashes, no em dashes** in prose we author (LaTeX `---` is
   fine inside the papers; it typesets an em dash but matches the
   established paper style - do not "fix" it).
3. **Rebuild PDFs in the same commit as any .tex change**
   (`tectonic -X compile <paper>.tex --outdir .`). Committed PDFs must
   never lag committed sources.
4. **Cross-paper references are hardcoded numbers** (e.g. Paper II
   cites "Paper I, Thm. 11.1"). If you renumber anything in Paper I,
   grep the other two papers for `Thm.~` and `Theorem~` and re-verify
   against the fresh `.aux` file.
