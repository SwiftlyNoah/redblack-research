\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We define the safe belief-carrying agent (one-round gadget re-solves at
every V2 boundary) and prove the composition inequality its certificate
meter computes: the agent's real-game exploitability is bounded by the
root solve's round-local exploitability plus, along the worst reachable
boundary--type chain, the sum of per-resolve \emph{gadget margins} --
quantities the resolver measures live with one exact best-response
sweep each. The bound uses no opponent model. We then state exactly
what the exact-frontier and functional-leaf-pricing work items owe the
certificate: per class and per member of the rank-1 factored entry
family, a promise vector that is \emph{certified honorable} together
with its functional sandwich gap. Every constant and both lemmas are
re-derived numerically by \texttt{simulator/scripts/v6\_verify\_composition.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting}

Heads-up Red/Black in the V2 decomposition (Paper 1;
\texttt{docs/solver.md}): play is cut at \emph{boundaries} -- procurement
resolutions with the loser's discard pending. All values are seat-0
payoffs in $[-1,+1]$; $U=2$ is the payoff range. A boundary $b$ is a
full public history $h_b$ ending at such a cut; $\kappa(b)$ is its
entry class, $T'(b)$ its post-discard layer. Private types are
$(r,x)$ pairs; $S^0_b, S^1_b$ the type grids of $\kappa(b)$;
$K_b(s,t)$ the deck-coupling kernel (\texttt{entry\_weights2}), which
is support-complete for every reachable joint (Paper 1). Measured on
$17{,}140$ boundaries of stored-profile self-play (v6 phase 0), every
reachable joint belief factorizes exactly as
\[
\mu_b(s,t) \;\propto\; K_b(s,t)\, g(s)\, \bar g(t),
\qquad \text{rank-1 residual} \le 3.3\cdot10^{-16},
\]
for some factor pair $(g, \bar g)$. Two warnings about these factors,
both measured. First, they are \emph{not} the seats' standalone
likelihood products: the layer prior $K_b$ is a different kernel at
every class (residual-pool hypergeometric), and composing per-seat
likelihood products against it misses the kernel drift -- the naive
product joint differs from the true posterior by TV up to $0.129$ on
played traces. Second, none of that matters to the certificate,
because of the following.

\begin{lemma}[model-free conditionals]\label{lem:cond}
For a fixed opponent strategy $\tau$ and public history $h_b$, the
true conditional distribution of the agent's type given the
opponent's type factorizes as
$\Pr(s \mid t, h_b) \propto K^{\mathrm{deal}}(a(s), a(t)) \, L_A(s)$,
where $a(s) = r + x$ is invariant along every transition, $L_A$ is
the agent's own likelihood sum, and $K^{\mathrm{deal}}$ is the initial
deal coupling. In particular it does not depend on $\tau$; and the
joint-filter update preserves it under \emph{any} strictly positive
opponent transfer, because $a$-invariance makes the coupling kernel
constant along both seats' transitions.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The deal couples the seats only through $(a_0, a_1)$, which are
deterministic invariants of the current types ($r$ drops by one and
$x$ rises by one on a red discard; nothing else moves either). Path
masses therefore factorize per seat given the coupling, and the
opponent's factor multiplies whole columns, cancelling from every
conditional. For the update claim: every own transition preserves
$a(s)$ and every opponent transition preserves $a(t)$, so the kernel
factor pulls out of both transfer sums unchanged.
Gate: \texttt{tests/test\_solver\_resolver.py::%
test\_support\_filter\_conditionals} (support-only opponent transfers
reproduce the exact filter's conditionals to $10^{-10}$ on played
traces).
\end{proof}

Everything the certificate consumes below is a $K g$-weighted
\emph{conditional} per opponent type, so by Lemma~\ref{lem:cond} the
agent's belief plumbing (\texttt{solver/beliefs.py} with the
own-solution override; byte-exact replay gate in
\texttt{tests/test\_solver\_resolver.py}) supplies the exact,
opponent-model-free quantities, and a break of the opponent-likelihood
filter is repaired by swapping the opponent transfer for its
structural support indicator without touching any conditional (the
safe resolver's fallback).

\paragraph{The agent.} Fix the agent seat $p$ and let $q$ be the
opponent. The safe continual re-solver $A$ plays:
\begin{itemize}
\item at the game root, the stored root-class solve $\sigma_{\mathrm{root}}$
  (root entry weights are the exact deal distribution; leaves priced by
  the stored leaf functional $\hat v$ below);
\item at every subsequent boundary $b$ it reaches, the round strategy
  $\sigma_b$ extracted from the \emph{gadget solve} of
  Definition~\ref{def:gadget}, then re-solves at the next boundary.
\end{itemize}
The leaf functional $\hat v_b(b',s',t') \in [-1,1]$ prices the
round-end leaf of a resolve at $b$ that lands on successor boundary
$b'$ with successor types $(s',t')$. The current implementation uses
the stored \texttt{v\_types}; items 3--4 replace it with certified
library envelopes (Section~\ref{sec:owe}). Terminal leaves
(elimination) are exact: $\hat v = \pm 1$.

\section{The gadget}

\begin{definition}[safe-resolving gadget]\label{def:gadget}
At boundary $b$ with own range $g_b$ (exact) and any strictly positive
weighting $\nu_b$ on $S^q_b$, the gadget game $\tilde G(b)$ is: chance
draws $(s,t)$ with weight $K_b(s,t)\,g_b(s)\,\nu_b(t)$; the opponent
observes $t$ and chooses \textsc{out}, ending the game at seat-0 value
$w_b(s,t)$ (the \emph{promise matrix}), or \textsc{in}, after which
the one-round subgame of $\kappa(b)$ is played with round-end leaves
priced by $\hat v_b$. The agent never opts out.
\end{definition}

In implementation (\texttt{cfrplus.\_ECSolve(gadget=(q, w))}) the
\textsc{out} action is one extra pre-stage action per opponent type
with counterfactual mass $\sum_s K_b(s,t) g_b(s) \nu_b(t) w_b(s,t)$;
the compiled in-round sweeps are untouched. Define the mass
$M_b(t) := \sum_s K_b(s,t) g_b(s)$, the \emph{conditional promise}
\[
\hat w_b(t) \;:=\; \frac{\sum_s K_b(s,t)\, g_b(s)\, w_b(s,t)}{M_b(t)},
\]
and for any agent round strategy $\sigma$ and opponent behavior
$\tau$, the opponent's \emph{conditional counterfactual value}
$\tilde V^{\tau}_q(b,t;\sigma)$: the $q$-signed expected payoff of the
gadget conditional on $t$, counterfactual over $s \sim K_b(\cdot,t)
g_b(\cdot)$. Note $\nu_b$ cancels from every conditional quantity; it
is a convergence weighting only.

\begin{lemma}[margin identity]\label{lem:margin}
Let $\sigma_b$ be the agent's (average) strategy from the gadget solve
and let
\[
m_b(t) \;:=\; \max_{\tau} \tilde V^{\tau}_q(b,t;\sigma_b) - \hat w_b(t)
\]
be its measured per-type margin (one exact best-response sweep;
\texttt{gadget\_out} in \texttt{solver/resolver.py}). Then $m_b(t)\ge 0$,
and for every opponent behavior in the gadget,
$\tilde V^{\tau}_q(b,t;\sigma_b) \le \hat w_b(t) + m_b(t)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The best response includes the \textsc{out} action, whose conditional
value is exactly $\hat w_b(t)$, so the maximum is at least
$\hat w_b(t)$; the inequality is the definition of the maximum. The
sweep is exact: the opponent's in-round information sets are (public
node, own private state) pairs and the sweep takes a per-state argmax
against path-summed agent reach, then a per-type maximum over
pre-stage choices including \textsc{out}
(\texttt{\_pre\_stage\_br\_types}).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[carry-down consistency]\label{lem:carry}
Construct promises and prices from one functional $\hat v$: at every
resolve, $w_b := \hat v$ on the entry grid of $\kappa(b)$, and leaves
priced by $\hat v_b$. Let $b'$ be a successor boundary reached from
the resolve at $b$, $g_{b'} = g_b \otimes$ (the agent's round transfer
under the \emph{played} $\sigma_b$). Then the conditional promise of
the next resolve equals the conditional leaf price the $b$-resolve
charged:
\[
\hat w_{b'}(t') \;=\;
\frac{\sum_{s'} K_{b'}(s',t')\, g_{b'}(s')\, \hat v_b(b',s',t')}{M_{b'}(t')} .
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Both sides are the same $g_{b'}$-weighted average of the same matrix:
the resolver's filter propagates its own factor with the
own-solution override, i.e.\ with exactly the played $\sigma_b$
(byte-exact shadow gate), and the opponent factor cancels from the
conditional as noted above. The verification script checks the
identity to float precision on played traces.
\end{proof}

\section{The composition theorem}

\begin{assumption}\label{ass:full}
Full carry: every non-terminal boundary the agent reaches is resolved
(gate $T'\le 9$). A depth-gated agent adds, per unresolved round, an
uncertified belief-reset term; the certificate is a full-carry object.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}\label{ass:break}
No unrepaired filter break. Enforced by construction in safe mode: on
a zero-likelihood opponent trace the agent swaps the opponent
transfer for its structural support indicator and keeps resolving,
which preserves every conditional the certificate consumes
(Lemma~\ref{lem:cond}) and cannot itself break (feasible public
contributions always have structural support, and the agent's own
transfer is positive along its own play). Gate:
\texttt{test\_safe\_shadow\_survives\_filter\_break}.
\end{assumption}

For opponent type $t$ at reachable boundary $b$, let $\CBV_A(b,t)$ be
the supremum over opponent play below $b$ of the opponent's true
conditional counterfactual value in $G$ against the agent's
continuation (resolve at $b$, play $\sigma_b$, re-solve onward), with
the same $K_b(\cdot,t)\,g_b$ counterfactual weighting. Reachable
chains are sequences $(b_1,t_1) \to (b_2,t_2) \to \cdots$ of
successor boundaries and opponent types under the agent's public
dynamics; every play path crosses exactly one boundary per round, and
a game has at most
\[
L_{\max} = 8
\]
resolved boundaries ($T' = 9, 8, \ldots, 2$; re-derived by script).

\begin{theorem}[composition, measured form]\label{thm:comp}
Under Assumptions~\ref{ass:full} and \ref{ass:break}, for every
opponent strategy $\tau$:
\[
u_q(A, \tau) \;\le\; B^{\mathrm{root}}_q \;+\;
\pathmax{(b_1,t_1)\to\cdots\to(b_L,t_L)} \;\sum_{i=1}^{L} m_{b_i}(t_i),
\]
where $B^{\mathrm{root}}_q$ is the opponent's exact best-response
value against the stored root tables in the $\hat v$-priced root
round, and the maximum ranges over reachable chains. Consequently,
for the mirrored pair $(A^{(0)}, A^{(1)})$:
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pair}} \;:=\; u^{\mathrm{BR}}_1(A^{(0)}) + u^{\mathrm{BR}}_0(A^{(1)})
\;\le\; \varepsilon_{\mathrm{round}}(\mathrm{root}) \;+\;
\sum_{\text{seats}} \pathmax{\text{chains}} \sum_i m_{b_i}(t_i).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Bottom-up induction on boundaries. Define
$D(b,t) := m_b(t) + \max_{(b',t') \text{ reachable from } (b,t)} D(b',t')$
(empty maximum $=0$).

\emph{Claim: $\CBV_A(b,t) \le \hat w_b(t) + D(b,t)$.}
Base: a resolve whose leaves are all terminal has $\hat v$ exact, so
the gadget game \emph{is} the truth below $b$ restricted to
\textsc{in}, and Lemma~\ref{lem:margin} gives
$\CBV_A(b,t) \le \hat w_b(t) + m_b(t)$.

Step: fix $\tau$ below $b$. The true conditional value decomposes over
the round's leaf aggregates: writing $\rho_\tau(b',t' \mid b,t)$ for
the opponent-and-chance reach into the aggregate of leaves landing on
$(b',t')$ (normalized so that $\sum_{(b',t')} \rho_\tau \le 1$;
each realization crosses one leaf),
\[
V^{\tau}_q(b,t) \;=\; \tilde V^{\tau}_q(b,t;\sigma_b)
\;+\; \sum_{(b',t')} \rho_\tau \left[\, V^{\tau}_q(b',t') -
\frac{\sum_{s'} K_{b'} g_{b'} \hat v_b(b',s',t')}{M_{b'}(t')} \right],
\]
because the gadget and the truth differ only in leaf pricing, and the
agent's reach into each leaf under $\sigma_b$ is exactly what defines
$g_{b'}$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:carry} the subtracted term is
$\hat w_{b'}(t')$; by induction
$V^{\tau}_q(b',t') \le \CBV_A(b',t') \le \hat w_{b'}(t') + D(b',t')$.
So the bracket is at most $D(b',t')$, hence
$V^{\tau}_q(b,t) \le \tilde V^{\tau}_q + \max_{(b',t')} D(b',t')
\le \hat w_b(t) + m_b(t) + \max D = \hat w_b(t) + D(b,t)$
by Lemma~\ref{lem:margin}. Taking the supremum over $\tau$ proves the
claim.

Root: the same decomposition applied once to the stored root round
(prior-weighted over root types, $\sum_t P(t) = 1$) gives
$u_q(A,\tau) \le B^{\mathrm{root}}_q + \sum_t P(t) \max_{\text{chains from } t} \sum m
\le B^{\mathrm{root}}_q + \max_{\text{chains}} \sum m$; the root's
leaf prices are the same $\hat v$ the first resolves promise
(Lemma~\ref{lem:carry} applies with $\sigma_{\mathrm{root}}$).
Pair statement: the two agents share the one stored root solve
seat-swapped, and by the meter identity
$B^{\mathrm{root}}_0 + B^{\mathrm{root}}_1 =
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{round}}(\mathrm{root})$ (sum of both
best-response values in a zero-sum game equals the sum of gains).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The bound is opponent-model-free: $\nu_b$ and the filtered opponent
factor influence \emph{which} equilibrium the resolve finds and hence
how small the margins come out, never the validity of
Theorem~\ref{thm:comp}. This is the property unsafe resolving lacks.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[what the margins measured today say]
With $\hat v = $ stored \texttt{v\_types}, per-resolve per-type margin
maxima of $0.08$--$0.39$ were measured at $T' \le 9$ on the first safe
full-carry games -- the live image of phase 0's bracket finding
(stored $T'{=}3$ values sit outside exact brackets by up to $0.146$,
reach-weighted $1.36\cdot 10^{-2}$). The measured-form certificate is
honest but loose exactly where the promises are dishonorable, and it
covers only \emph{visited} boundaries; closing both is items 3--4.
\end{remark}

\section{What items 3--4 owe the certificate}\label{sec:owe}

Theorem~\ref{thm:comp} charges measured margins along the worst
\emph{reachable} chain, but exploitability quantifies over all
opponents, including ones steering to boundaries never visited in any
probe. The resolver there is deterministic-but-uncomputed, so the
certificate needs an a-priori bound on $m_b(t)$ valid at \emph{every}
reachable $(b, g_b)$. This is precisely the frontier's job, in the
functional form phase 0 forced. Items 3--4 owe, for every gated
boundary class $\kappa$ and every member $g$ of its rank-1 factored
entry family:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{An honorable promise vector} $w_\kappa(g, \cdot)$: a
  certified guarantee that some playable round strategy (a library
  entry, extended on demand) caps the opponent's true conditional CFV
  below the boundary at $w_\kappa(g,t) + r_\kappa(g,t)$ for all $t$
  simultaneously, with certified residual $r$. At frontier layers
  ($T' \le 3$ today, $T' \le 4$ where built) the library sandwich is
  exact in $G$ and $r = 0$ up to the certified cover gap; above the
  frontier $r$ recurses through this same table.
\item \textbf{The functional sandwich gap}
  $\Delta_\kappa(g,t) := w_\kappa(g,t) - \ell_\kappa(g,t)$, the
  certified distance between the library's upper and lower envelopes
  \emph{evaluated at the resolve's own entry ranges} (two exact-BR
  sweeps per entry, $\sim$5\,ms). This is the leaf-error norm of
  record -- median holdout gap $2$--$4\cdot 10^{-3}$ versus $0.26$--$0.50$
  for static per-pair intervals -- and it bounds both the promise's
  conservatism (head-to-head cost) and its overshoot.
\item \textbf{An off-path enforcement rule}: the safe resolver
  compares its solve's measured margins against the capping entry's
  and plays the better, so that
  $m_b(t) \le \varepsilon_b + r_{\kappa(b)}(g_b, t)$ holds at
  \emph{unvisited} boundaries by construction, where $\varepsilon_b$
  is the per-resolve convergence residual.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{corollary}[certified form]\label{cor:cert}
With items 1--3 in place,
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pair}} \;\le\;
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{round}}(\mathrm{root}) \;+\;
\sum_{\text{seats}} \pathmax{\text{chains}}
\sum_{i=1}^{L \le L_{\max}} \bigl[\, \varepsilon_{b_i} +
r_{\kappa(b_i)}(g_{b_i}, t_i) \,\bigr],
\]
every term computed offline or recorded live. This is the quantity
\texttt{scripts/composed\_cert.py} (item 5) will print next to the
probe lower bound.
\end{corollary}

\section{Verification}

\texttt{simulator/scripts/v6\_verify\_composition.py} re-derives:
$L_{\max} = 8$ from the layer ladder; Lemma~\ref{lem:carry} as a
float-precision identity on played traces; and Theorem~\ref{thm:comp}
end-to-end on a $T'{=}3$ class -- it transcribes the composed agent
(gadget resolve at the boundary + per-history gadget resolves at the
$T'{=}2$ successors) into the flattened perfect-recall oracle
(\texttt{solver/exact.py}), computes the opponent's exact per-type
best response against the composed strategy, and checks it against
$\hat w + $ the chain-sum of recorded margins. The oracle merges
procurement flip interleavings (order-irrelevance, Paper 1), so the
verified agent variant feeds its filter order-canonicalized records;
this changes nothing in the theorem, which never references flip
order.
